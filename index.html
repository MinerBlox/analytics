Traffic & referral attribution

Designed for date-wise source snapshots

No traffic source is connected. After the RepsCentral admin metric is confirmed, daily website visits can be stored as timestamped snapshots and aligned with Hipobuy invite/active deltas.

Video attribution needs either unique tagged links or a platform export that reports clicks/conversions per post. A same-day correlation can show movement together; it cannot establish that a video caused it.
